  \label{tab:upstream-bugs}

    \toprule
    Where & Defect & Effect \\

        link survived. \\
    \addlinespace
    \textbf{INET QUIC} & \textsc{reset\_stream} released stream-level but not connection-level
      flow-control credit for abandoned bytes (RFC 9000 §4.5)
      & The connection window leaked shut on every reset. \\
    \addlinespace
    \textbf{INET QUIC} & Send-side flow control decremented on loss and never re-charged
      retransmissions (RFC 9000 §4.1)
      & Sender overran the peer's limit; connection aborted with
        \textsc{flow\_control\_error} at small windows. \\
    \bottomrule
  \end{tabular}
